# Chapter 3: The Crossing

The western gate was guarded by two sentinels draped in heavy iron armor. Mist swirled around their boots as the midnight bell tolled across the city.

Lira and Kael slipped quietly into the shadow of the old aqueduct. With a whisper of ancient words learned from the manuscript, the stone archway shimmered softly, opening a hidden passage behind the ivy-covered wall.

They stepped through into the wild expanse beyond Aethelgard—where the ancient forests met the starry horizon. Their adventure had officially begun.
