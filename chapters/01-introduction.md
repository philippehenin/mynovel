# Chapter 1: The Whispering Library

The rain fell in steady, rhythmic sheets over the slate roofs of Aethelgard. Inside the High Archive, the smell of aged parchment and beeswax filled the quiet air.

Lira leaned over the heavy oak desk, her magnifying lens highlighting faint, glowing runes beneath the faded ink of an ancient codex. For centuries, scholars had assumed this manuscript was merely an astronomical logbook. But tonight, beneath the pale moonlight filtering through the high arched window, something unexpected caught her eye.

The ink was breathing.

She paused, holding her breath as a subtle shimmer rippled across the page. A sequence of symbols shifted position, aligning into a map—a path leading far beyond the known borders of the continent.
