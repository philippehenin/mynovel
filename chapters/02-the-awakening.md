# Chapter 2: The Awakening

Before Lira could record the coordinates, a low hum vibrated through the stone floor beneath her feet. The crystal pendant around her neck flared with warm amber light.

"Lira, you shouldn't be here after curfew," whispered Kael, stepping silently from the shadow of the tall bookshelves.

Lira gestured toward the desk without looking up. "Look at this, Kael. It's not an astronomical log. It's a key."

Kael leaned closer, his skeptical expression turning to awe as the glowing runes cast soft patterns across the dark woodwork. "If the High Council discovers you've unsealed this text..."

"Then we don't have much time," Lira replied decisively, closing the leather-bound book and slipping it into her satchel. "We leave tonight."
