# Chapter 12: Out of the Labyrinth

The emergency tunnel plunged into a gallery carved straight out of the Sandstone Peaks rock. Behind them, steam bursts and rumblings drowned out the guards' shouts.

Lira and Kaelen ran through the dark for nearly twenty minutes, guided only by the rumble of thermal water underfoot. Their shoulders brushed in the dark, bound by an unwavering trust.

Suddenly, a gust of fresh air, crisp, freezing, and free of any coal stink, slapped their faces. The tunnel opened into a natural arch carved in the mountainside.

They emerged into the open air at daybreak. Before them lay the breathtaking view of Shadowvale: a wild valley dotted with the grand ruins of First Era geothermal stations. In the distance, Aethelgard's black roofs looked tiny under their blanket of soot.

« We made step one », Lira said, taking in the view with an exhausted but victorious smile. Kaelen looked at her, his warm brown eyes filled with quiet tenderness.

« Then let's not waste time », he whispered softly. « The mountain is waiting. »
