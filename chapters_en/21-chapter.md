# Chapter 21: Showdown Under the Dome

The Cloud Observatory Dome was an unequaled masterpiece of Precursor celestial architecture. Thousands of adjustable rock-crystal lenses tracked and refracted sunlight, mounted on gigantic frames of amber bronze that rotated silently in harmony with Earth's rotation.

At the center of the rotunda stood the main control desk for the continental geothermal network.

Suddenly, a crash of shattered glass echoed beneath the vault. Inquisitor Thorne, winched down from a final attack airship stabilized above the peak, dropped through the shattered dome glass and landed on the upper walkway, shock rifle raised.

« It's over, Vance! » Thorne bellowed, his voice echoing with desperate passion beneath the crystal vault. But meeting Lira's unwavering dignity and calm, the old sergeant's finger on the trigger wavered. « Don't break the balance... You'll destroy us all! »

« Balance isn't silence or ignorance, Thorne! » Lira replied, her voice clear, firm, and filled with unshakeable truth. « Balance is shared harmony! Look around you: this grid was built to heal Aethelgard, not destroy it! »

Thorne hesitated, eyes brimming with tears and doubt. In that moment of faltering, Kaelen lunged forward to knock the rifle barrel aside.

The electric blast struck a brass beam in a shower of blue sparks. While Kaelen disarmed Thorne without unneeded violence and helped him to his feet, Lira sprinted to the rotunda's main control desk. She slotted in Aldous Vance's ferromagnetic memory cartridge and pulled down the main gold resonance lever with all her strength.

« Global geothermal grid activation! » she called out.

A beam of pure, sparkling golden light shot instantly from the peak of the crystal dome. The beam cut across Aethelgard's sky from east to west, piercing the soot clouds and illuminating the entire valley and city in a comforting, warm, and eternal glow.

Thorne, overwhelmed by the transcendent beauty of the restored light and finally understanding the truth, collapsed to his knees and dropped his copper mask in a sob of relief.
