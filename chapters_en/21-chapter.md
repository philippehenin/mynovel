# Chapter 21: Showdown Under the Dome

The Cloud Observatory Dome was a marvel of celestial engineering. Thousands of rock crystal lenses tracked the sky, mounted on bronze frames that rotated with the Earth.

But Inquisitor Thorne, winched down by a final attack airship over the dome roof, dropped through the shattered glass, shock rifle raised.

« It's over, Vance! » he called out. But meeting Lira's unwavering, dignified gaze, his voice wavered. « Don't break the balance... »

« Balance isn't silence, Thorne! » Lira replied, her voice ringing clear. « It's shared harmony. »

Kaelen lunged forward to knock the rifle off target. The electric discharge hit a brass beam in a shower of blue sparks. While Kaelen disarmed Thorne without unneeded violence, Lira sprinted to the main control desk and slotted in the ferromagnetic memory card.

« Global geothermal grid activation! » she called out, pulling down the main gold lever.

A beam of pure light shot from the dome's peak, cutting through Aethelgard's sky and lighting up the whole valley. Moved to tears by the beauty of the restored light, Thorne dropped his copper mask.
