# Chapter 2: The Breathing Ink

A golden wave rippled across the foxed vellum of the Codex. The characters, usually frozen in grim calligraphy, slid smoothly across the page with a faint metallic chime, like a distant tuning fork taking a hit. Lira gripped her quill holder with cold, sweaty fingers. The lines drawn by forgotten scribes recomposed themselves right before her eyes, charting out the complex map of a sky and ground grid.

The route shot straight toward the forbidden zones of Shadowvale and the sheer cliffs of the Sandstone Peaks. She drew a gray cloth notebook from her desk. Careful not to ruin the paper's reactive surface, she began transcribing the unknown constellations and the geothermal tuning frequencies. Each number matched the exact pressure setting of a boiler from the old days.

« If the Council catches what I'm looking at », she whispered under her breath, « they won't even wait for sunrise to send the sweepers. » Legend had it that the Order of Silence had burned whole blocks of the Lower Town just to wipe out the trace of a single ferromagnetic dial.

Suddenly, a sharp crack echoed from the upper balcony level. A long shadow stretched across the wrought-iron railing of the third tier. Lira snapped the Codex shut, stuffed it into her reinforced canvas satchel, and blew out her oil lamp. Darkness swallowed her like a protective cloak.

In the pitch black of the Archives, only the distant hum of High City turbines remained. Lira held her breath, her back pressed against the solid oak shelves. The heavy boots of the night watch drew near, lantern beams leading the way. The adventure had just plunged into complete secrecy.
