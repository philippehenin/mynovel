# Chapter 14: The Engineers' Relay

The secondary pumping station of the Engineers' Relay, once known as the Sandstone Manor, stood like an elegant fortress of dressed red sandstone blocks and reinforced glass windows at the crossroad of alpine valleys. Despite two centuries of abandonment and solitude, the building retained a proud stature, protected from time by its exceptional masonry and the gentle warmth of the underlying thermal spring.

Kaelen pulled a thin steel pick from his tool kit and worked the rusted latch on the rear delivery door. The heavy iron-clad oak panel gave way with a sigh of oxidized gears.

Inside, the grand machine hall was bathed in solemn silence and a timeless atmosphere. Long testing benches of precious teak, white porcelain pressure dials graduated in amber ink, and large master control levers of solid copper lay intact under a thin veil of golden dust.

Lira immediately sat at the main control desk and unrolled the *Aethelgard Codex*.

« Look at the dials on the north wall, Kaelen! » she exclaimed, watching the porcelain needles quiver with steady motion. « Pure water pressure released from the Four Waters Hub is already filling the relay's underground reservoirs! »

Kaelen unbuttoned his jacket, rolled up his sleeves, and immediately set to work on the auxiliary steam turbine mechanism. Using a rag soaked in cleaning ether, he scrubbed brass injectors and lubricated bronze bearings.

« If I can realign the intake regulators », he explained, soot on his forehead but eyes gleaming with technical fervor, « this station will generate enough hydraulic thrust to power up the grand mechanical lift clinging to the Sandstone Mountains. That'll save us a brutal three-day climb on foot. »

Suddenly, a low, rhythmic thrum—like the beating of metal wings high up in the clouds—made the reinforced glass panes shiver. Council attack airships had just entered Shadowvale's airspace.
