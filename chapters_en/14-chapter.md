# Chapter 14: The Engineers' Waystation

The secondary pump station at the Engineers' Waystation stood like a red sandstone and reinforced glass fortress where the valleys met. Though empty for centuries, the building had held up remarkably well against the weather thanks to master masonry.

Kaelen picked the rusty latch on the delivery door. Inside, the massive turbine hall was silent as a tomb. Teak test benches, white porcelain dials, and solid copper levers lay under a thin blanket of gray dust.

Lira sat at the main desk and spread out the Codex map. « Pressure from the Hub of Four Waters is already reaching the accumulators », she noted, watching the gauges drift toward green.

Kaelen went straight to work on the main turbine, clearing the injectors with an ether-soaked rag. « If I can get this station humming, we'll have enough juice to run the mechanical lifts up the Sandstone Peaks. »

Outside, the distant thrum of airship engines warned that Council patrols would soon be sweeping the valley. They had to work fast and clean.
