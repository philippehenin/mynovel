# Chapter 7: The Militia Raid

A massive shock slammed into the workshop's front door, shearing steel pins and rattling tool-laden shelves. The hammered-iron panels buckled inward under the devastating impact of a Council militia pneumatic ram. Shards of glass flew across the room in sharp showers, shattering the bell jars protecting precision escapements.

« In the name of the Council of Silence, open up! » bellowed a harsh voice amplified through a copper horn outside. It was the imperious voice of Inquisitor Thorne. « Seize the heretical tools, confiscate the contraband, and eliminate the traitors! »

A second blow from the pneumatic ram tore away the main lock in a burst of bluish sparks.

Kaelen didn't hesitate for a fraction of a second. With a swift, practiced movement, he grabbed the *Aethelgard Codex*, stuffed it into a waterproof tarred canvas bag, sealed it tight, and slung the shoulder strap over Lira. Then he grabbed her hand with unshakeable resolve.

« Through the purge hatch! Move, Lira! » he yelled over the din.

The reinforced doors buckled and tore open with a deafening metallic crunch. Half a dozen Council militia guards, clad in heavy black leather coats and armed with shock rifles, stormed into the shop, their copper masks gleaming under the spotlight beams.

Kaelen lunged toward the side wall and yanked a large red cast-iron lever with all his strength.

A high-pressure steam purge valve burst open with a deafening hiss. An opaque, scalding white cloud of pressurized water and ether steam erupted from the wall pipes, instantly drowning the workshop in blinding fog. Shouts of rage and confusion broke out among the guards, followed by blind shockfire blasts that split the steam in blue sparks and shattered mahogany furniture.

Taking advantage of total chaos, Kaelen lifted a heavy cast-iron plate hidden beneath his main workbench. A steel ladder descended into the gaping darkness of the city's industrial basements.

« Jump! » Kaelen roared.

Lira plunged into the opening, gripping the cold rungs as electric discharges crackled over her head. Kaelen followed at breakneck speed and slammed the heavy iron plate shut just as an electric blast struck the edge of the hole in a blinding flash of ozone. The silence of the earth closed over them.
