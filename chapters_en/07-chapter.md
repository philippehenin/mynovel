# Chapter 7: The Militia Raid

A massive shock slammed into the workshop's front door. The wrought-iron panels buckled under the hit of a militia pneumatic ram. Shards of glass flew across the tool racks.

« In the name of the Council of Silence, open up! » bellowed the amplified voice of Inquisitor Thorne. « Seize the forbidden tools and eliminate the heretics! »

Kaelen didn't hesitate. He grabbed the Aethelgard Codex, stuffed it into a waterproof tarred bag, strapped it to his back, and took Lira's hand with steady assurance. « Through the purge hatch! Move! »

A second pneumatic blast shattered the main lock. Militia guards in black leather coats and armed with shock rifles stormed into the shop. Kaelen yanked a wall lever: a high-pressure steam valve burst open, flooding the room in a thick, scalding white cloud.

Shouts of rage and blind shockfire echoed through the artificial fog. Taking advantage of the chaos, Kaelen lifted the heavy iron plate under his workbench and dropped into the underground depths, pulling Lira right behind him before the hatch slammed shut.
