# Chapter 9: The Aqueduct of the Ancients

After nearly an hour of exhausting progress through the stifling darkness of the purge conduits, the steel chute suddenly opened onto a monumental cavern. Lira and Kaelen emerged onto a bronze grating catwalk suspended from the face of a subterranean cliff, a hundred and fifty feet above a shadowy abyss.

The sheer surprise rooted them to the spot. The dynamo lantern beam swept an expanse of breathtaking grandeur: it was the Aqueduct of the Ancients, a forgotten underground cathedral built centuries before Aethelgard was ever founded.

Gigantic pink granite pillars, carved with perfectly regular geometric fluting, rose from the abyss to support an immense basalt vault draped with ochre stalactites and limestone crystals. Far below in the gorge, a powerful thermal river boiled and roared with a majestic echo, sending up plumes of white steam that caressed the stone catwalks.

« It's... it's magnificent », Lira whispered, awestruck by the architectural majesty of the place.

« Look at the south wall », Kaelen replied, aiming the light beam across the chasm.

Colossal pipes forged of amber bronze, wide as steam locomotives, ran along the rock walls, forming interconnected junction hubs of fascinating complexity. Titanic cast-iron valves, ten-meter gear wheels, and control handwheels coated in fine mineral moss lay dormant there, frozen in time since the Great Collapse.

It was the beating heart of Precursor energy engineering. This network of purified hot water and steam had once powered the entire metropolis with pure energy, long before the founding fathers of the Order of Silence locked the conduits and plunged the city into the era of soot and coal.

Lira pulled the *Aethelgard Codex* from its waterproof pouch and opened it against the catwalk railing. Under the influence of the humid air and thermal heat rising from the underground river, the reactive ink on page three hundred and forty-two glowed with newfound intensity.

« If we can reach the regulation station at the Four Waters Hub », Kaelen said, studying the glowing lines, « we can realign the relief valves and unlock the geothermal pressure. But the passage is guarded by Council listening posts. »

Lira adjusted her monocle with renewed resolve. « Then we'll slip past their posts. Aethelgard's freedom is well worth crossing this aqueduct. »
