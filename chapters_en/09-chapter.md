# Chapter 9: Aqueduct of the Ancients

After an exhausting hour crawling through overheated conduits, the steel tube suddenly opened into a dizzying void. Lira and Kaelen stepped onto a metal grating walkway suspended high above a massive chasm.

It was the Aqueduct of the Ancients, a forgotten underground cathedral built beneath the Lower Town. Giant pink granite pillars supported a basalt vault where orange limestone stalactites hung down. At the bottom of the abyss, a river of thermal water roared, carrying plumes of steam.

« Look over there », Lira said, pointing her lantern at the opposite wall. Massive bronze pipes, wide as train cars, ran along the rock in complex knots.

Titan-sized clockwork mechanisms, thirty-foot gears covered in moss, had been frozen for three centuries. It was the beating heart of Aethelgard's ancient engineering, abandoned during the Great Collapse.

« If we can realign the main station valves », Kaelen grinned, « we can restore pressure to the whole grid without touching the Council's coal plants. But to do that, we gotta reach the Hub of Four Waters. »
