# Chapter 11: Showdown at the Hub

Blue electrical blasts crackled against the walkway handrails. Council guards fired their shock rifles at the Hub platform.

« Get behind the main control block! » Kaelen yelled, shielding Lira with his own body against the cast-iron frame.

Inquisitor Thorne stepped up to the top of the stairs, his copper mask gleaming under the shockfire glare. But instead of shooting, Thorne slowly removed his mask, revealing a face scarred by pain and eyes filled with an old grief.

« Lira Vance... », Thorne said, his voice breaking. « You think I guard the coal out of greed? My own brother burned alive in the explosion of the first geothermal reactor built by your great-grandfather! This energy is a ticking time bomb! »

Lira stood frozen by the tragic revelation. Then, glancing at the pressure gauge calibrated by Aldous, she realized: « My great-grandfather didn't destroy the grid, Thorne. He built the harmonic valves specifically to prevent that explosion! Your blind fear is suffocating the city! »

Kaelen cranked release valve number three. A protective cloud of warm steam rose between them and the guards, opening the path to Shadowvale.
