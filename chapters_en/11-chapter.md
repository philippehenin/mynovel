# Chapter 11: Showdown at the Hub

Blue electrical discharges crackled menacingly along the bronze handrails of the walkway. Firing shock rifles, Council guards hammered the central vessel of the Four Waters Hub, ripping away showers of bluish sparks and sending red-hot shards of iron flying through the air.

« Take cover behind the main control block! » Kaelen roared, shoving Lira against the reinforced frame to shield her with his own body.

Inquisitor Thorne stepped slowly into view at the top of the stone stairs, flanked by four elite guards with rifles raised. His black leather coat glistened with condensation under the spotlight beams. But just as the militia prepared to fire, Thorne raised a iron-gloved hand to command a halt.

Then, with a deliberate and solemn motion, he unlatched the fittings of his hammered copper mask and gently pulled it off.

For the first time, Lira saw Sergeant Thorne's true face. It was the face of a man in his fifties, aged prematurely, scarred by a deep burn mark running from his left temple down to his jawline. His dark, hollow eyes were filled with an ancient, boundless pain.

« Lira Vance... », Thorne said, his voice breaking, retaining none of its usual harshness. « Do you truly believe I serve the Council of Silence and defend coal out of pure greed or love for tyranny? »

He took a step toward the platform's edge, pointing down at the vibrating geothermal pipe beneath their feet.

« My own younger brother, Julian, was nineteen when he served as Aldous Vance's assistant in these very tunnels », Thorne continued, his voice trembling with emotion. « He burned alive in the runaway explosion of the first experimental reactor! Your great-grandfather tried to harness a force beyond human understanding. This amber energy is no gift from the Ancients: it's a ticking time bomb that will destroy the entire city if unleashed! »

Lira stood frozen for a moment, struck to the core by the revelation of this personal tragedy. But taking a clear look at the precision gauges Aldous had calibrated and the harmonic schematics on page three hundred and forty-two, the truth shone with unassailable clarity.

« My great-grandfather didn't cause your brother's death out of recklessness, Thorne! » Lira replied, her voice ringing with conviction. « The reactor blew because the Council abruptly cut the relief valves to sabotage the demonstration! And Aldous didn't destroy the grid: he designed these harmonic valves specifically to prevent that tragedy from ever happening again! Your blind fear and the dogma of Silence are suffocating the city and killing children in the bas-fonds! »

Before Thorne could respond, Kaelen yanked the main discharge lever on valve number three with both hands.

A dense, protective cloud of pure water steam erupted with a colossal roar between the platform and the guards. Blinded and repelled by the thermal surge, the guards faltered. Seizing the opening, Kaelen and Lira ducked into the south drainage tunnel, escaping toward the wild expanses of Shadowvale.
