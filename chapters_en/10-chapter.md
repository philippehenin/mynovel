# Chapter 10: The Four Waters Hub

The bronze grating catwalk gave an eerie creak with every step across the thermal chasm. Lira held fast to the green-patinated handrails, keeping her eyes fixed on the opposite bank to avoid the dizzying roar of the boiling water a hundred and fifty feet below.

At the end of the suspended pathway rose the monumental structure of the Four Waters Hub. It was an octagonal dome of massive amber bronze embedded directly into the basalt rock, pierced by four titanic conduits sealed with handwheel valves.

At the exact center of the main circular platform stood the acoustic tuning console.

It was the identical twin of the console drawn on page three hundred and forty-two of the *Aethelgard Codex*. The carved brass base featured resonant crystal inlays and custom-milled rectangular slots designed to receive the manuscript binding.

« This is where my great-grandfather worked », Lira whispered, poignant emotion in her voice as she touched the bronze controls.

She pulled the Codex from its waterproof pouch and slotted it into the central receptacle. The fit was surgically precise: a heavy, cavernous mechanical click echoed through the underground chamber, immediately followed by the lighting of a row of amber crystal indicators that diffused a soft golden glow.

« The resonant footprints align perfectly », Lira declared, carefully turning the master brass harmonic selector.

The four gigantic valves quivered in a chorus of steel groans. Plumes of superheated, purified steam erupted from the joints with a powerful, majestic hiss. The granite platform swayed gently beneath their boots, vibrating at an infrasonic frequency of fifty hertz that resonated in Lira's very bones.

« Geothermal pressure is surging back through the subsoil! » Kaelen exclaimed, eyes sparkling with enthusiasm as he worked the flow equalizer levers. « If we open the Shadowvale junction valve, we can purge the toxins from the Lower Town! »

But their moment of victory was brutally shattered.

A beam of piercing blue electric light cut through the fog at the top of the aqueduct stairs. A volley of shockfire slammed into the catwalk railing in a crash of sparks and brass shrapnel.

« Seize them! Don't let them reactivate the dome! » yelled Thorne's voice through the rising steam.
