# Chapter 10: The Hub of Four Waters

The grating walkway creaked under their boots with every step. Lira held fast to the rusty handrails, trying not to look down into the boiling void. Ahead of them rose the structure of the Hub of Four Waters: an oxidized bronze dome pierced by four giant geothermal valves.

At the center of the main platform stood the acoustic tuning desk, the exact twin of the one shown on page three hundred and forty-two of the Codex. Lira pulled the book from her bag and set it onto the brass pedestal.

« The geometric marks match down to the millimeter », she declared, turning the desk's central dial. A heavy clunk echoed throughout the subterranean cavern.

The four massive valves shuddered. Plumes of superheated steam burst from hemp seals with an ear-splitting hiss. The granite floor shook at a deep, low frequency that made Lira shiver to her core.

« It works! » Kaelen yelled, adjusting the pressure levers. But their relief was short-lived: a blue light beam cut through the mist at the top of the aqueduct stairs. The militia had tracked them down.
