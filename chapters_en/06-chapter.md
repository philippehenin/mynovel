# Chapter 6: The First Tuning Fork

Kaelen carefully opened a crimson velvet case hidden at the back of a mahogany drawer. From it, he retrieved an exceptionally crafted tuning fork forged from a secret alloy of amber bronze and pure silver by his own grandfather. The stem of the tuning fork bore a watermark of the same winged gear crest engraved on Aldous Vance's family ring.

« My grandfather always told me that the right frequency could bend metal », Kaelen whispered, bringing the instrument close to the open book.

He struck the steel prongs cleanly against the side of his precision vise. A pure, crystalline note, vibrating at a frequency of four hundred and thirty-two hertz, rose through the warmth of the workshop.

The effect on the *Aethelgard Codex* was instant and breathtaking.

The note didn't fade: it was picked up and amplified by the very structure of page three hundred and forty-two. Copper pipes hanging from the workshop ceiling hummed in resonance, emitting a harmonic vibration that made the green felt on the workbench shiver. Before Lira and Kaelen's spellbound eyes, the ink of synthetic amber and copper powder sprang into frantic motion.

The once-stationary lines unfolded like the metallic petals of a liquid mechanical flower. The outlines of Shadowvale and the Sandstone Mountains lit up with a brilliant golden glow, revealing three primary geothermal bypass valves sealed beneath the stone.

« Look at this », Kaelen said, his voice trembling with emotion near Lira's ear. « This isn't just a hydraulic schematic. It's the tuning guide for a global geothermal harmonic resonator. The Precursors didn't burn a single ton of coal: they harnessed the Earth core's natural oscillation to radiate heat and light into every household in Aethelgard! »

Lira recorded every coordinate in her gray cloth notebook, her lead pencil flying across the paper.

« And the Council of Silence dismantled this grid to force a monopoly on coal », she concluded, her teeth set in restrained fury. « They'd rather poison thousands of children in the Lower Town than lose an ounce of their tyrannical power! »

Suddenly, the tuning fork's crystalline note was brutally shattered by a deafening roar outside.

The high-pitched wail of steam sirens from militia airships tore through the night. At the same instant, blinding yellow searchlights from three attack vessels pierced the workshop's dirty glass windows, sweeping across the red brick walls and casting long, menacing shadows over the tool racks. The tuning fork's resonant signal had just been picked up by the Council watchtower's acoustic sensors.
