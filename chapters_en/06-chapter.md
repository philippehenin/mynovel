# Chapter 6: The First Tuning Fork

Kaelen pulled a precision tuning fork made of silver-bronze alloy from his tool chest, a gift forged by his grandfather. He tapped it lightly against the edge of the table and set the vibrating stem right onto page three hundred and forty-two of the Codex.

The result was instant. A pure, crystal note rang out in the workshop, immediately picked up by the copper pipes hanging from the ceiling. The amber and copper dust ink came alive, redrawing the subterranean pipeline grid of the city before their amazed eyes.

« Check this out », Kaelen muttered, his voice close to Lira's ear. « This isn't standard piping. It's a geothermal harmonic resonator. The Ancients didn't burn coal to heat the city; they tapped the Earth's core vibration! »

Lira jotted frantic notes in her notebook. « And the Council of Silence buried the tech to keep their coal monopoly and hold the people in sickness and cold », she concluded bitterly.

Suddenly, a sharp siren outside drowned out the tuning fork's chime. Pale searchlights from militia airships swept across the grimy stained glass, casting giant shadows on the brick walls. The patrol had just picked up the acoustic signal.
