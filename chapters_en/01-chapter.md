# Chapter 1: Rain Over the High City

The autumn rain was coming down hard over Aethelgard's slate roofs, drumming steadily against the High City's copper gutters. Deep inside the Grand Archives, a dizzying stack of dark oak shelves climbing all the way to the basalt vaults, it smelled of linseed oil, old dust, and cold bergamot tea. Tucked behind her heavy study desk, Lira Vance adjusted her double-lens brass monocle. At twenty-four, trapped in a suffocating bureaucratic routine, she felt stifled under the Council of Silence's thumb.

Down in the damp alleys of the Lower Town, the Amber Cough was taking a toll on working-class children, poisoned by toxic coal fumes. Lira couldn't look away anymore. Under the weak flicker of her oil lamp, she flipped through the Aethelgard Codex, a thick volume bound in rough leather, worn down by the years. For three centuries, the Academy's top brass had filed this log away with old, worthless star catalogs. But Lira knew her great-grandfather, Aldous Vance, had hidden the truth inside it.

She ran a finger over the geometric symbols stamped onto the cover, feeling the faint hum of a hidden metal frame right under the dry hide. That night, under the pale moonlight cutting through the lancet windows, one detail caught her eye. Pressing her magnifying glass against the bottom corner of page three hundred and forty-two, she saw the paper's grain was nothing like standard vellum.

The page shimmered with microscopically fine gold threads. Lira brought the flame closer, checking the lines from different angles. The ink wasn't made from ordinary soot, but a tight mix of synthetic amber and pulverized copper dust. Under the warmth of her breath, the pattern started moving. The ink was breathing.

Lira got goosebumps. This codex wasn't just a bunch of ancient papers; it was a live receiver under voltage, a piece of working machinery crafted by First Era engineers. Every calligraphed letter hid a metallic micro-circuit capable of catching the Earth's vibrations. She held her breath, heart pounding, determined to break the silence and save her people.
