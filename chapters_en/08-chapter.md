# Chapter 8: Inside the Purge Conduit

The fall ended abruptly on a smooth copper chute slick with wet soot. Lira and Kaelen landed flat on their bellies at the bottom of a circular pipe barely four feet wide, bathed in pitch blackness and stifling heat.

The sound of militia boots and wave detectors faded above them, replaced by the deep, hollow roar of the city's guts. It smelled of stagnant water, sulfur, and hot metal.

« You alright? » Kaelen whispered, cranking his hand-dynamo torch. His hand rested reassuringly on Lira's shoulder for a moment. The dim yellow light lit up the pipe walls covered in green oxidation crust.

« Other than my knees and my pride, I'm good », Lira winced, adjusting her monocle with a faint smile. « But if we stay stuck here, the purge steam will cook us alive in ten minutes. »

Kaelen checked the flow of warm air. « The conduit slopes south toward the old drainage galleries beneath the aqueduct. If the Codex map is right, we can reach the main valve hub without going back to the surface. » They started crawling on all fours through the metal tube.
