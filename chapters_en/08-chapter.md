# Chapter 8: In the Purge Conduit

The dizzying descent ended abruptly on a smooth copper chute, worn slick by decades of runoff water and liquid soot. Lira and Kaelen tumbled full length onto the floor of a circular conduit barely four feet in diameter, bathed in absolute darkness and stifling humidity.

Above their heads, on the other side of the heavy sealed iron plate, the commotion of militia boots, Thorne's barked orders, and the crash of splintered furniture faded away, gradually replaced by the deep, powerful, continuous thrum of Aethelgard's mechanical underbelly.

The air smelled of reducing sulfur, warm synthetic grease, stagnant water, and white-hot metal.

« Lira? Can you hear me? Are you whole? » Kaelen's anxious voice whispered in the dark.

He clicked the mechanical trigger of his small pocket dynamo lantern. The gear tooth ratchet hummed briefly, then a carbon filament bulb cast a beam of trembling yellow light. Kaelen placed a calloused hand on Lira's shoulder with gentle care to make sure she wasn't hurt. The glow illuminated the circular conduit walls, covered in a greenish patina of copper oxidation and long streaks of condensation.

« Aside from my knees, my ruined coat, and my pride as an archivist, I'm fine », Lira grimaced, adjusting her monocle with a brave half-smile in the dim light. « But the bag is dry, and the Codex is safe. »

She glanced at the wall pressure gauge mounted a few feet away on oxidized metal. The red needle quivered dangerously in the warning zone.

« On the other hand », she added, wiping a drop of dirty water from her forehead, « if we stay trapped in this pipe, the automatic boiler purge will cook us alive in under ten minutes. »

Kaelen crawled forward and inspected the conduit's slope. He pulled a small gyroscopic compass from his pocket and laid it against the copper sheet.

« The warm draft flows south, toward the abandoned drainage tunnels beneath the Lower Town's grand aqueduct », he explained with pragmatic calm. « If Aldous's map on page three hundred and forty-two is accurate, this purge channel empties directly into the inspection chamber of the Four Waters Hub. That's our only way out to escape the city without facing militia checkpoints on the surface. »

Without losing a second, clutching the canvas bag tight, they crawled deeper into the metal chute, scrambling through the muggy heat beneath the city of men.
