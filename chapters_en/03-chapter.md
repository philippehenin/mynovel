# Chapter 3: The Council's Cops

The hobnailed boots of the Council's cops echoed against the slate tiles with the rhythm of a guillotine blade. Lira crouched in the blind spot of an aisle stacked with tax registers from the ancient dynasty. The pale beam of a kerosene lantern swept the leather bindings above her head, stirring up decades of settled dust.

« I heard a noise back in the star chart section », grunted a harsh voice, Sergeant Thorne's. Lira knew the man's reputation: under his copper mask, Thorne was a grief-stricken soul, convinced that ancient energy brought nothing but destruction. « Search the alcoves. If it's another apprentice digging for forbidden secrets, haul them straight down to the purge pits. »

Lira tightened the strap of her satchel. The Codex felt heavy against her ribs, but abandoning the book was out of the question. She slipped along the woodwork without a sound, using the dark to reach the binders' ventilation turret.

A cast-iron air hatch, caked with coal dust, sat at the foot of the eastern wall. Lira picked the brass latch with the tip of her pocketknife. The metal squeaked faintly, but the crash of guards knocking over stacks of files across the hall covered the noise. She squeezed into the narrow chute just as a cone of light flooded her old desk.

The cold night air slapped her face as she emerged onto the outer ledge. Down below, the Lower City spread out in a maze of tin roofs and smokestacks belching black soot. The High City had just closed behind her; her only lifeline lay down in the damp alleys of the lower quarters.
