# Chapter 5: The Clockmaker Street Workshop

The wrought-iron door creaked open on ungreased gears. Kaelen's soot-streaked face appeared in the gap, a heavy wrench in hand and safety goggles pushed up on his forehead. Recognizing Lira, his usually calm eyes filled with sharp concern and quiet respect.

« You're crazy coming here at this hour, Lira », he whispered, gently but firmly yanking her inside before slamming the three steel deadbolts. « The Council militia did two raids on the street since sunset. They're hunting contraband books. »

Kaelen's shop was a haven of underground ingenuity and warmth. A comforting scent of mineral oil, ether, and black tea filled the room. Brass pipes ran along brick walls, steam gauges twitched under pressure, and acoustic test benches occupied the center.

Lira dumped the satchel onto the oak workbench and pulled out the Codex. The rough leather gleamed under the swinging acetylene lamp hanging from the ceiling. Kaelen stepped up, set his tool down, and carefully peeled off his thick leather gloves, their hands brushing for a second.

« I pulled it out of the Archives », she said, catching her breath. « And it's no star catalog, Kaelen. It's the full blueprint of Aethelgard's geothermal grid. The ink reacts to vibrations. » The young mechanic leaned over the book, captivated.
