# Chapter 13: Crossing the Vale

Shadowvale stretched before them like a forgotten sanctuary of pink stone, steam, and rusted metal, where wild nature had patiently reclaimed its rights over the ruins of the First Era. Colossal steel lattice pylons, covered in fine silver lichen and creeping acacia vines, reared their majestic frames into the milky morning sky.

Lira and Kaelen picked their way along the red sandstone banks of the thermal riverbed.

The steaming water wound between rock boulders, radiating a gentle, comforting warmth that stood in stark contrast to the icy wind blowing down from the snowcapped peaks of the Sandstone Mountains. Wild flowers with amber-blue petals—species unheard of in the polluted High City—bloomed along the warm water's edge.

« My great-grandfather used to tell me about Shadowvale when I was a boy », Kaelen shared, eyeing the mossy hulk of an ancient maglev engine half-buried in brambles. « He said engineers of old piped heat up to the citadel without producing a single puff of combustion smoke. Down in the Lower Town, the elders brushed off those stories as fairy tales to lull apprentices to sleep. »

Lira kept the *Aethelgard Codex* open in her left hand, guiding their march with her brass compass and tracking the micro-variations of the reactive ink at every elevation level.

« They were no fairy tales, Kaelen », she replied, pointing to a structure of red brick and amber bronze silhouetted at the edge of the plateau. « The next anchor point is the Sandstone Manor, the old Engineers' Relay. That's where Aldous Vance kept his personal journal before the Council banished him. »

Rounding a narrow rocky defile, the hulks of thermal centrifuges and steam vehicles lay abandoned in the valley. Irrefutable proof of Aethelgard's past grandeur lay tangible before their eyes, waiting only for a courageous hand to rekindle the spark of knowledge.
