# Chapter 13: Crossing the Vale

Shadowvale was a wilderness of stone and rust where nature had reclaimed the First Era ruins. Giant steel pylons, eaten away by lichen and wild vines, reached their skeletons into the pale morning sky.

Lira and Kaelen picked their way along the thermal riverbed. The steaming water wound between red sandstone boulders, releasing a gentle warmth that fought off the icy mountain wind.

« My grandfather used to talk about this valley », Kaelen said, eyeing the remnants of an old pneumatic line. « He said the pioneers ran power all the way to the citadel without burning a single lump of ore. »

Lira kept checking the Codex maps, tracing their path with her brass compass. « Next anchor point is the Engineers' Waystation. That's where the secondary pump house is located. »

Around a rocky pass, the skeletons of old steam tractors lay half-buried under brambles. The world before wasn't a myth: it was a lost civilization waiting for a push to come back to life.
